Privacy Policy for Map Timeline Tool

Map Timeline Tool may access the following user data on your device:

- Precise and approximate location, when you use map, point, and location-related features. When you enable the optional Quick Add notification, the app may use a short-lived precise-location cache; on Android 10 and later, keeping that cache available in the background requires the background-location permission.
- Photos, when you attach photos to points or include photos in an export or backup.
- Microphone input, when you enable noise level measurement for a point.

How we use this data

- Location data is used to display maps, record points, and support location-related features. Quick Add saves a point only when it has a recent accurate cache entry or can obtain a fresh precise fix.
- Photos are used only to associate images with your saved points and to include them in exports or backups when you choose to share your data.
- Microphone input is used only to calculate an approximate dBFS noise value when you save a point with noise detection enabled; it is not a calibrated sound-pressure measurement.

Data handling

- The app processes location, photos, and audio locally on your device.
- We do not sell user data.
- We do not upload this data to our own servers.
- When you choose to export or share a backup, the exported file may contain point data, location data, tags, photos, sensor values, and settings metadata, and it is shared only to the destination you select.
- Microphone audio is not recorded as a voice file, saved as an audio file, or retained after the noise calculation is complete.
- If Android system backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may back up or transfer app-sandbox data through the backup destination configured on your device.

Third-party services

- The app may request map tiles from third-party map providers in order to display maps.
- These providers may receive network requests associated with map viewing, such as tile requests from your device.

Security

- User data is stored locally on your device and protected by Android's app sandbox.
- Exported files are shared only through the sharing action you initiate.

Data deletion

- You can delete saved points, photos, tags, and settings from within the app.
- If you remove the app, locally stored app data is removed from the device according to Android system behavior. Any copies stored by your configured system-backup provider are governed by that provider's retention and deletion controls.

Contact

- If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the app developer.

By using the application, you agree to this data usage policy.
