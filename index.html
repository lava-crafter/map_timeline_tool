Map Timeline Tool

An offline-first Android app for manually logging points. Save coordinates, time, notes, tags, and photos on your device.

▶ GET IT ONGoogle Play View source on GitHub →

Version 0.1.9 · Android 7.0 and up

FEATURES

Point logging, maps, and data export.

⌖

Log points

Record a location with a title, note, tags, timestamp, and optional photo.

▧

Map and list views

Browse saved points on a map or in a list. Cached and downloaded tiles can be reused offline.

⇄

Import and export

Import CSV or ZIP. Export CSV, GeoJSON, KML, KMZ, or a full ZIP backup.

DATA

Stored locally on your device.

Points, photos, and settings are kept in the app sandbox. Use ZIP backups when you want to move or keep a copy of your data.
